204 Runs, 100M Tokens: Which Agent Can Keep a Knowledge Base?

30 models & 13 harnesses put to the test · Jul–Oct 2026

Method and problems met · All 204 runs (CSV)

For some years I have used an Obsidian vault as my knowledge base: insights, decisions, people, tasks, ideas. Can an agent keep it up to date? I recorded a real meeting (the other participant agreed beforehand to the recording and the AI processing) and let agents update my vault, putting the right insights in the right files and folders.

Vault
~200 files, ~15K lines
Transcript
46 min of ambiguous, garbled French speech-to-text, ~30K tokens
Instructions
a one-line prompt, but a 145-line vault guide and a skill to file the insights
Context
agents search the vault, working context 50–95K tokens
Expected
4 destinations: a Journal entry, a meeting note, idea notes, a contact profile: right folder, frontmatter, tags, links
Score
out of 100: half note quality, half judgment, minus penalties (details)

Verdict: local works, if you give it time

Some frontier cloud models get it right in 1–6 minutes, but many other cloud models fall short. Locally, only one setup gets it right reliably, in about 15 minutes: the plain Qwen3.8 27B, 4-bit, extra-high effort, on Pi. Fine-tunes hit the top once, then fall back.

    Why it's a difficult bench

    • Garbled input. Every line is echoed twice, because the microphone picked up both my voice and the speakers (no headphones). Speaker labels are unreliable; acronyms and names are mangled.
    • But recoverable. The vault holds the ground truth about me, so an agent can attribute each statement to the right person.
    • Ambiguous direction. The call was between peers. Read carelessly, the follow-up (who introduces whom) flips: runs got it right.
    • Similar background. We share some family background, so the two biographies blur: runs mixed them up or invented one.

    Other learnings

    Harnesses do matter

    The harness is the program around the model: its prompt, tools and limits. It decides whether a run finishes, and the same model can score 30 points apart on two harnesses. Which harness works best depends on the model.

    No harness wins everywhere. Pi was best for Claude Sonnet 5.5 and the self-hosted models, OpenCode 2 for Qwen3.8 Flash Next; GPT-6 Luna and DeepSeek did well on all of them. The other harnesses mostly stalled or failed their tool checks.
